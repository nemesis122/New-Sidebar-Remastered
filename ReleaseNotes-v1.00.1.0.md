# Sidebar Remastered by NemeSIS v1.00.1.0

Portable Windows x64, .NET included. **EXE and matching source ZIP are both attached.** A small readability update for v1.00.0.0.

## Fixed

- **Labels on bright fills:** on dark color themes, the label and value inside each glass capsule now have a soft dark halo. Labels such as "Load", "Temp" or "Clock" stay readable over bright liquid, most visibly in Smoke Glass. Light themes keep their text unchanged.
- **Settings window:** the title at the top of the navigation shows "Sidebar Remastered" instead of a cut-off "Sidebar Remastered by Ne…".

## Download and update

Download **Sidebar-Remastered-v1.00.1.0.exe** and replace the previous EXE; keep `remastered-settings.json` beside it so your settings carry over. **Sidebar-v1.00.1.0-Source.zip** is the matching source. The EXE is unsigned. See **VALIDATION.txt** and **SHA256SUMS.txt** for test scope and integrity.
