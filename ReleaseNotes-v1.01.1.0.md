# Sidebar Remastered by NemeSIS v1.01.1.0

Portable Windows x64, .NET included. **EXE and matching source ZIP are both attached.**

## Fixed

- **Glass Columns:** cards with fewer than three bars (for example drives) switch to slim tubes. Those tubes now have the normal 7 px thickness at 100 % instead of 24 px.

Includes everything from v1.01.0.0: the new Glass Tiles, Glass Columns, Glass Ring and Glass Inline designs, and the translucent Smoke Glass.

## Download and update

Download **Sidebar-Remastered-v1.01.1.0.exe** and replace the previous EXE; keep `remastered-settings.json` beside it so your settings carry over. **Sidebar-v1.01.1.0-Source.zip** is the matching source. The EXE is unsigned. See **VALIDATION.txt** and **SHA256SUMS.txt** for test scope and integrity.
