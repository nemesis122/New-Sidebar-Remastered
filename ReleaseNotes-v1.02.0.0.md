# Sidebar Remastered by NemeSIS v1.02.0.0

Portable Windows x64, .NET included. **EXE and matching source ZIP are both attached.** Three new widgets and a design of your choice for every widget.

## New: design per widget

Under **Appearance & text → Design per widget** every widget gets its own design, for example the clock as a Glass Ring, the CPU as Glass Columns, RAM as Glass Tiles and the drives as Glass Inline. "Same as above" keeps the card layout chosen for the whole sidebar. The page's reset button also clears these choices.

## New widgets

| Widget | Shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Clock** | Time as the large number, the date as its subtitle, day progress and calendar week. In Glass Ring the ring shows the day progress. |
| **System** | Uptime as the large number, processes, threads and handles. |
| **Battery** | Charge, charging state and remaining time. Appears only on devices with a battery. |

The clock joins your existing card order at the top, system and battery after the hardware widgets. Show, hide or reorder them under **Cards & order**, and pick their accent color under **Text & charts**.

## Download and update

Download **Sidebar-Remastered-v1.02.0.0.exe** and replace the previous EXE; keep `remastered-settings.json` beside it so your settings carry over. **Sidebar-v1.02.0.0-Source.zip** is the matching source. The EXE is unsigned. See **VALIDATION.txt** and **SHA256SUMS.txt** for test scope and integrity.
