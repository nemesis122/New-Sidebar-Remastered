# Sidebar Remastered by NemeSIS v1.03.0.0

Portable Windows x64, .NET included. **EXE and matching source ZIP are both attached.** Three additional designs in three different directions; 13 designs in total.

## New designs

| Design | Character |
| --- | --- |
| **Acrylic Widgets** | In the style of desktop widgets: the hardware icon in a tinted chip, the large number with its trend over the last ten seconds (▲ / ▼) and a 60-second sparkline beside it, thin bars on shared column edges. |
| **Liquid Glass 2.0** | More transparent glass with a stronger sheen, borderless capsules with a polished inner highlight, larger numbers, and fill levels that glide to each new reading. Windows' "Show animations" setting turns the gliding off. |
| **Clean Dashboard** | Flat and calm: no sheen, a subtle border, a divider under the large number and hairline bars under each reading. |

All three follow every setting (colors, text sizes and visibility, line/bar thickness, graphic size, warning colors, light themes) and can be chosen per widget under **Cards & order → Design per widget**.

## Download and update

Download **Sidebar-Remastered-v1.03.0.0.exe** and replace the previous EXE; keep `remastered-settings.json` beside it so your settings carry over. **Sidebar-v1.03.0.0-Source.zip** is the matching source. The EXE is unsigned. See **VALIDATION.txt** and **SHA256SUMS.txt** for test scope and integrity.
